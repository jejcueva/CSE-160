Assignment 3: Fox Spirit Grove

A first-person virtual world built with textured cubes, a perspective camera, keyboard and mouse controls, editable block stacks, and the fox from Assignment 2 placed in the world as the shrine guardian.

Controls: W / A / S / D move, Q / E rotate, drag on the canvas to look around, F removes a block, and G adds a block.

Reference used: Matsuda/Lea WebGL Programming Guide from assgn3CONTEXT/references.txt, especially Chapter 5 for textures and Chapter 7 for camera setup.

Preparing the grove.

Quest: Recover the 3 spirit flames hidden across the grove.
